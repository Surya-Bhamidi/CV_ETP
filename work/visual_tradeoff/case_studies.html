Sharper-looking faces and smaller reconstruction error are different goals

A smoother output can score better on PSNR/SSIM while a generative model produces a sharper-looking face. Neither metric alone proves the original identity was recovered. Larger models are not guaranteed to win a different objective or a particular degradation.

These are the same 128 validation images already examined. No new training or accuracy improvement occurred. The illustrations below were selected after measuring LPIPS and include strengths and counterexamples. They are not random test examples or evidence of universal superiority.

Answer for the presentation

Our network has 0.48 million parameters and runs faster in this measured crop pipeline. It achieves higher PSNR/SSIM under the declared degradation, while the heavier models achieve better perceptual-distance scores on average and can look better. Different training objectives and input conditions can change the ranking; more parameters do not guarantee a win on every metric. We have not demonstrated universally better identity recovery.

Training examples would measure fitting, not generalization. These examples are validation images and explicitly selected illustrations, not unseen test evidence. No new training or reconstruction improvement occurred in this analysis.
